About

Tools for users who run their own models

LLM SysCore builds open-source infrastructure software for self-hosted LLMs.

LLM SysCore started the way most useful infrastructure does: someone was running language models on their own hardware and got tired of juggling terminals, ad-hoc scripts, and half a dozen browser tabs to answer simple questions. Is the model up? How fast is it? What is this actually costing me?

The answer grew into LLM Systems Manager: a complete operations platform for self-hosted LLM infrastructure. It treats a homelab GPU rig, a Mac Studio in the office, and a rack of servers as infrastructure worth managing properly, with real telemetry, cost accounting, and one place to control it all.

Everything we ship is open source under AGPL-3.0, and everything runs on your hardware. No cloud dependency, no telemetry phoning home, no account required.

What we believe

Four rules we build by

Local-first

Your models, your data, and your metrics stay on your machines. The whole platform works on a network with no internet access at all.

Honest measurement

Costs come from measured watts and counted tokens, not estimates. When a number is based on partial data, the dashboard says so.

The operator stays in control

Nothing restarts, installs, or reroutes without your approval. The platform surfaces what it wants to change, and the operator decides when it happens.

Open source, in the open

Development, issues, and releases all happen publicly on GitHub.

What we build

The LLM SysCore stack

manager

LLM Systems Manager

The flagship: dashboard, OpenAI-compatible gateway, model library, benchmarking, autotuning, Autopilot routing, users and roles, backups.

agent

LLM Systems Agent

One cross-platform binary for Linux and macOS that auto-detects what each host runs and reports system, GPU, PSU, and inference metrics.

alarm-engine

LLM Systems Alarm Engine

A standalone service that stores every sample, evaluates threshold and anomaly rules, and notifies over email, webhook, toast, and Discord.
